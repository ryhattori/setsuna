described in Zhang, Y., Rózsa, M. et al. Fast and sensitive GCaMP calcium indicators for imaging neural populations.
*Nature* 615, 884-891 (2023).

Extracted from asset `jGCaMP8s/jGCaMP8s_ANM479572_cell08.nwb`, region of interest 0 (the neuron recorded with the
pipette): the somatic and neuropil fluorescence (`F`, `Fneu`), the frame times (`frame_times`, s), the imaging rate
(`fps`) and the loose-seal action-potential times (`spike_times`, s), unchanged. The movie and the other regions of
interest are not included.
